Privacy notice — Rockbox Gmail reader

Last updated: 22 October 2026. Operated by Susie Brown, trading as Backstage Systems (UK sole trader).

What the app does

The Rockbox Gmail reader is an internal tool. It signs in to ONE dedicated Gmail inbox, owned by Backstage Systems, which receives event-listing emails (venue newsletters and event alerts). It reads those emails, extracts event details (date, venue, act or event name, time) and labels each email as processed. No other Google account is accessed, and the app has no other users.

What data is used

  • The content of emails in that one inbox, to extract event details.
  • The extracted event details are stored in a private database used by Backstage Systems' client for internal planning. They are not published or sold.
  • To read each email, its text may be sent to an AI model provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) under their API terms, solely to extract the event details.

What is not done

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described above. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Emails stay in the inbox unless deleted by its owner. Extracted event details are kept while the client's service runs. To ask about or remove data, contact us.
